# VibeBuddy 1.3.21 — Mac, iPhone and Watch

- Mac 新增“设备与连接”中心，集中显示当前连接、配对二维码和外出访问设置；菜单栏可直接打开连接面板。
- iPhone 首页显示当前 Mac，点按即可检查连接或添加设备。首次连接与已有设备的入口保持一致。
- Mac 可将检测到的远程访问地址发送给已配对的 iPhone。手机验证连接与 Mac 身份后保存设置，并将结果回传给 Mac。
- 连接更新支持取消、过期和重试。失败时保留原有连接，修复失败后重启误报更新失效的问题。
- 保留 1.3.20 的共享内容风格和 iPhone 独立播报设置。

## English

- Open Devices & Connection on Mac to manage pairing codes, current connections and remote access. Reach the connection panel directly from the menu bar.
- Tap the connected Mac on iPhone to check its connection or add a device, with consistent entry points for first-time and existing users.
- Send a detected remote-access address from Mac to a paired iPhone. The phone verifies the connection and Mac identity before saving the address and reporting the result.
- Cancel, expire or retry connection updates while retaining the previous connection after a failure. Fix incorrect expired-update feedback after restarting from a failed attempt.
- Retain the shared content styles and independent iPhone speech settings introduced in 1.3.20.

Mac build 33. iPhone, iPhone widget, Watch and Watch widget build 48.
