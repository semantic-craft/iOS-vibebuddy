# VibeBuddy 1.3.20 — Mac, iPhone and Watch

- 新增统一内容风格：简洁、决策汇报和自定义提示词。通知摘要、朗读、历史摘要和回顾按用途生成合适篇幅。
- iPhone 新增“摘要与播报”，可修改当前 Mac 的内容风格，并独立选择手机播报服务、音色和主播风格。
- 支持重播原稿和按当前风格重新生成。更换音色或主播风格不打断正在播放的内容，朗读不会标记已读。
- 改进配置保存冲突、断线提示、试听取消和完成原文读取，避免旧请求覆盖新设置或新播放。
- 保留本地密钥与直连服务商模式。Mac 内容生成和手机云端合成使用各自配置的服务；系统朗读无需云端合成服务。

## English

- Choose a shared content style: concise, decision briefing, or a custom prompt. Notifications, speech, history summaries and recaps use lengths appropriate to each purpose.
- Use Summary & Speech on iPhone to edit the connected Mac's content style and choose the phone's speech provider, voice and delivery style independently.
- Replay the original script or regenerate with the current style. Changing the voice or delivery style leaves current playback running, and listening never marks a result as read.
- Improve save conflicts, offline feedback, preview cancellation and completed-result reading so stale requests cannot overwrite newer settings or playback.
- Keep provider keys on their respective devices and connect directly to configured providers. System speech does not require a cloud speech service.

Mac build 32. iPhone, iPhone widget, Watch and Watch widget build 47.
