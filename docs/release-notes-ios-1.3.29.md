# VibeBuddy 1.3.29 for iPhone and Apple Watch

## 简体中文

- 手机锁屏时，也能在手表上把正在跑的任务「停下」。
- 手机锁屏之后才开始的任务，现在打开手表 App 就能看到，不用先点进某个任务。
- 手表横幅上的「回复」会直接打开回答卡片，在卡片上选答案或口述。
- 任务详情页的「返回总览」在任务已经离开列表时也能用；列表每一行都标出是哪个 agent。
- 手表上的回答会绑定到当时那个问题；问题换了，就不会误答到新问题上。
- 语音：只有你在这一句里说出任务名，批准、拒绝、回答、指示才会执行；通话时长到顶时会说明原因，并可一键重拨。Gemini 语音已移除。

## English

- Stop a running task from the Watch even while the iPhone is locked.
- A task that started after the phone was locked now shows up as soon as you open the Watch app, without opening a task first.
- Reply on a Watch banner opens the answer card, where you can pick an answer or dictate one.
- "Back to dashboard" works even when the task has left the list, and every row now names its agent.
- Answers from the Watch are tied to the question they were given for, so a replaced question is never answered by mistake.
- Voice: approve, deny, answer and instruct only go through when you name the task in that sentence. A call-length limit now ends the call with a reason and a one-tap redial. Gemini voice has been removed.

iOS build 59. The accompanying Mac release is 1.3.33 (51).
