# VibeBuddy 1.3.31 for iPhone and Apple Watch

## 简体中文

- 现在可以接收 Mac 发来的同一 Wi-Fi 局域网连接，也可继续使用 Tailscale 在外连接。新地址验证成功后才会保存，失败时保留原来的连接。
- 灵动岛最小显示增加环形状态标记，更容易分辨任务状态。
- 改进连接方式和切换结果的说明。

## English

- Receive a same-Wi-Fi local-network connection from your Mac, alongside existing Tailscale connections. A new address is saved only after validation succeeds; failed checks keep your previous connection.
- The minimal Dynamic Island display now includes a ring-shaped status indicator to make task states easier to distinguish.
- Clearer explanations of connection methods and switching results.

iOS / Watch build 64。配套 Mac 版本为 1.3.38（56）。
