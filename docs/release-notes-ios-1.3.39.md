# VibeBuddy iOS 1.3.39

## English

Task results no longer have read or unread states. Completed results remain available without adding to pending counts. Historical, read-only tasks no longer count as needing your attention, including past failures. Older historical tasks stay in history, while live questions and approvals remain visible.

## 简体中文

任务结果不再区分已读和未读，完成结果仍可查看，但不再增加待处理数量。历史只读任务（包括过去失败的任务）不再计入“需要你”；较早的历史任务归入历史记录，当前等待回答或批准的任务仍保留提醒。
