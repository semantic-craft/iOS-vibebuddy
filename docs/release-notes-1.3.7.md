# vibebuddy 1.3.7

## Mac

- Filter menu sessions by state and agent, collapse groups, and clear the current Done & Idle entries from the menu. Clear keeps unread results and reminders; a new round can reappear.
- Optional Grok Bot observation and separate account quota, with a dedicated source icon. Verified ordinary completions can use the existing AI summary and opt-in serial read-aloud queue. Grok Bot remains read-only: handle its questions in the official Mac app. Unverifiable question continuations remain degraded rather than being reported as successful.
- Approval controls now consistently respect whether the current request can be answered remotely. Native-only waits clearly direct you back to the Mac.

## iPhone and Apple Watch companion — 1.3 (17)

- Share consistent approval availability and handling instructions across the dashboard, Live Activity and Watch.
- Identify followed Watch tasks by their agent source, including Grok Bot, while preserving completion summaries and exact-round read acknowledgements.
- Display the Mac-provided Grok Bot quota alongside other supported providers.

Menu filters, collapse and Clear are local presentation preferences. They do not delete source sessions or mark results read on other devices. Device notification and audio preferences remain independent.
