# VibeBuddy 1.3.34 — macOS

- 无障碍：开着 VoiceOver 时，提醒改发系统通知并会被朗读，不再只是几秒后就消失的刘海卡片（没开通知权限时仍退回卡片）。刘海、速览和面板里的内容能被 VoiceOver 完整读出，批准和选中的选项会被播报。
- 深色模式下薄荷绿按钮上的白字对比度从 2.06:1 提高到 9.09:1；打开「增强对比度」后，细线和浅灰字会变深。
- 打开「减少动态效果」后，刘海的弹簧动画和循环动效会停下。
- 发到手机和手表的审批提醒，160 字以内的命令会完整显示，更长的加「…」（原来只显示前 120 字，而且不带省略号）。

Mac build 52。配套的 iPhone / Apple Watch 版本为 iOS 1.3.29（61）。

## English

- Accessibility: with VoiceOver on, alerts arrive as system notifications that are read aloud, instead of notch cards that vanish after a few seconds (they fall back to the card when notifications are off). VoiceOver reads the notch, glance and dashboard in full and announces approvals and chosen options.
- White text on mint buttons in dark mode goes from 2.06:1 to 9.09:1 contrast; with Increase Contrast on, thin lines and light grey text get darker.
- With Reduce Motion on, the notch's spring animation and looping effects stop.
- Approval alerts sent to the phone and Watch show commands of up to 160 characters in full and add "…" to longer ones (they used to cut at 120 with no ellipsis).

Mac build 52. The accompanying iPhone / Apple Watch release is iOS 1.3.29 (61).
